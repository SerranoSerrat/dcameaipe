Notable changes to the package, newest first.

·v0.1.0 — TODO: date

Initial release, accompanying the Sociological Science article.

  • dcame_aipe() — estimates the D-CAME and the AIPE from a joint model with group-specific functions of the treatment
  • Two ways to approximate the treatment function: basis regression with cross-validated selection over polynomials and splines (model = "basis"), and penalized GAM smooths via mgcv (model = "GAM")
  • Three inference paths — "crossfit", "bootstrap" (including block cluster bootstrap) and "regular" — so that model-selection uncertainty is carried into the standard errors
  • compare = "ALL" reports the linear interaction coefficient, the D-CAME and the AIPE side by side
  • Plot method combining predicted values, interactive partial effects and the estimand comparison, following Mize (2019)
  • Support for continuous moderators via tercile binning (basis) or a bivariate smooth (GAM), additive controls, fixed effects with optional fixest absorption, and survey weights
